**Title:** Flying NPC at the beginning of the game stops shooting when I am in another room. In the starting room and already the next one further, and no damage is dealt when approaching close at the border of the doorway.

**Project:** STAR TALE: Odyssey One
**Build version:** Latest at the time of writing.
**Platform:** PC, Steam, Windows 11
**Date:** 25.10.2026 1:02

**Severity:** High
**Priority:** Medium

**Short description:** 
The flying NPC stops shooting when the player is in another room. The NPC gets stuck at the doorway, and when approaching it at close range, it shoots but deals no damage.

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Loaded the game.
2. Provoked the flying NPC.
3. Ran to the door that opens without a key code.
4. The NPC flew up to the doorway and does not fly further, even when I hold the door open.
5. At the border of the doorway I approach close to the flying NPC, then he starts shooting, but there is no damage and the bullets pass through me.

**Expected result:**
The flying NPC should shoot at me even through the open door, and not just stand and wait for me. Damage should be dealt to me and bullets should not pass through me.

**Actual result:**
The flying NPC stands at the border of the doorway and waits for me, does not shoot. Damage is not dealt and bullets pass through me.

**Reproducibility:** 100%, frequency 5/5.

-  Video: [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/hCM0esArexg)


**Additional:**
- The walking NPC attacks immediately and attacks through the doorway.
- If the door closes, the NPC just stands and waits for me, the NPC's status does not change.
- At the border of the room he just stands and looks at me.
- Approaching close, he starts shooting but damage is not dealt and bullets pass through me.
- Aggression is not reset even upon re-entry.
