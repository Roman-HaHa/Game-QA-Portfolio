**Title:** [Visual/Collision] Ship geometry and textures disappear when jumping near the three left windows

**Project:** STAR TALE: Odyssey One
**Build version:** ID 25659831
**Platform:** PC (Steam), Windows 11
**Date:** 26.10.2026

**Severity:** Medium
**Priority:** Medium

**Short description:** 
In the starting room, when jumping on the three left windows, the ship's textures and geometry start to disappear. Only the space and background planets remain visible.

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Load the game and spawn in the starting room.
2. Turn towards the three left windows.
3. Approach them and jump onto the windows (the bug reproduces on all three).
4. Start jumping and observe the disappearing textures.

**Expected result:**
During jumps, all textures and ship geometry are displayed correctly without visual defects.

**Actual result:**
While jumping on the windows, the geometry breaks and textures disappear. Instead of the ship, only the planets and space are visible in the background.

**Reproducibility:** 100% (5/5). No dependency on the camera angle.

**Media:**
- Video: [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/pKq9uIV41YY)

**Additional:**
- The bug consistently reproduces after restarting the game (5/5).
- Reproduces exclusively on the three left windows, not observed elsewhere.
- Graphic settings: maxed out. Resolution: maxed out.
